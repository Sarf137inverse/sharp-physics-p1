## The Problem

Most textbooks are not bad because the authors didn't know the subject.
They are bad because nobody engineered them.

Typography, chapter structure, definitions, derivations — every decision was made by habit, by tradition, by eyeballing. Nobody asked why. Nobody had to. The student had no alternative, so the tax was never noticed.

The tax is real. Every redundant sentence, every wall of text, every derivation that follows historical convention instead of logical necessity — it costs the student time and cognitive energy. Multiply that across millions of students and thousands of hours. The damage is enormous and normalized.

Sharp Physics does not accept the tax.

---

## The Engineering Principle

Engineering means every decision is justified by a reason that can be stated out loud.

Not "it looks good." Not "that's how it's always been done." A reason.

Serif fonts guide the eye along the line — that is a reason. That single example defines the methodology. Before any decision is made in this book — typography, structure, derivation method, word choice, heading hierarchy — the question is asked: why? The decision does not proceed until the answer survives scrutiny.

This is the Unix philosophy applied to a book. Unix has one rule: do one thing and do it well. No redundancy. No decoration. Every component earns its place by function. Sharp has the same rule. Every word earns its place. Every figure earns its place. Every heading level earns its place. If it does not earn its place, it gets cut.

This is not minimalism as an aesthetic. It is minimalism as a discipline.

---

## The North Star

Every engineering decision needs a constraint. Without a constraint you are optimizing into the void.

The constraint for Sharp Physics is this:

*A student sitting in an exam hall must be able to recall everything in this book.*

That single constraint eliminates an enormous amount of bad textbook behavior immediately.

Walls of text — eliminated. The brain cannot retrieve from a blob.
Redundant explanations — eliminated. They dilute the signal.
Derivations that follow historical convention instead of logical clarity — eliminated. History is not a reason.
Jargon for its own sake — eliminated. If the word does not aid recall it is noise.

What survives the constraint is exactly what belongs in the book. Structure that maps to how memory works. Definitions precise enough to be retrieved under pressure. Derivations that follow logical necessity, not tradition. Typography that reduces friction between the eye and the brain.

When writing anything for this book, ask one question: will the student be able to recall this in the exam hall? If the answer is no, rewrite it until it is yes.

## The Development Model

This book is not a document. It is a system under continuous development.

SpaceX does not build a rocket and call it done. They iterate. They test. They find failure modes and eliminate them. The rocket gets better with every version. Sharp Physics operates the same way. Every chapter, every definition, every derivation is a component that can be improved, replaced, or cut. That is why it is on GitHub. That is why it has CI. That is why it has governance.

The goal is not to publish. The goal is to converge on something that cannot be improved further — knowing that convergence is asymptotic and the work is never finished.

---

## What This Means in Practice

If you are contributing to this book, you are not writing a textbook. You are engineering one.

Every decision you make must have a reason. If you cannot state the reason out loud, the decision is not ready. If the reason is "that's how it's usually done," the decision is wrong.

The reader is a student under pressure. Write for that student. Not for comprehensiveness. Not for the examiner. Not for tradition. For the student in the exam hall, trying to recall.
